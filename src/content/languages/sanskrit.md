The classical literary and liturgical language of the Indian subcontinent, encompassing Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500–500 BCE) and Classical Sanskrit (codified by Pāṇini, c. 4th century BCE). Sanskrit's prestige extended across South and Southeast Asia for over two millennia.
