The Greek language from Homer through Late Antiquity, spanning the Archaic, Classical, Hellenistic (Koine), and early Byzantine phases. The Attic dialect of Classical Athens became the prestige form, and its Koine descendant served as the lingua franca of the eastern Mediterranean for over a millennium.
