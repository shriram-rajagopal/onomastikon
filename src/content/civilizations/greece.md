The Greek case is a particularly rich illustration of the endonym–exonym gap. Hellenes called themselves *Héllēnes* from at least the Archaic period; their neighbors knew them by an array of names derived either from small local groups whose contact preceded broader knowledge of the Aegean world (Latin *Graeci* from the Boeotian *Graikoi*; Persian *Yauna* from the Ionians) or from later cultural transmission (Sanskrit *Yavana* loaned from Old Persian; Hebrew *Yāwān* from a Northwest Semitic intermediary).
