Loaned from Old Persian *Yauna* (same Ionian root), transmitted through Persian–Indian contact. Pāṇini (4.1.49, c. 4th c. BCE) mentions *Yavanānī*, a "Yavana script," indicating awareness of Greek writing. The term initially designated Greeks (especially the Indo-Greeks of Bactria and northwest India) but was later broadened in some texts to mean "Westerner" more generally.
