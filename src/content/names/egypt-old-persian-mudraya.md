The Old Persian name for Egypt, attested in Achaemenid royal inscriptions including the Behistun inscription and the lists of Darius I's subject lands. The form is a loan from a Semitic source related to Akkadian *Miṣru* and Hebrew *Miṣrayim*. Egypt was an Achaemenid satrapy from Cambyses' conquest in 525 BCE through the end of the Twenty-seventh Dynasty.
