Derived from *Ionia* (Ἰωνία); the Persians first encountered Greeks via the Ionian cities of western Anatolia and applied the term to all Greeks within their orbit. Attested in Achaemenid royal inscriptions including the Behistun and Naqsh-e Rustam inscriptions of Darius I.
