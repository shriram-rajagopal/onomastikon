From Greek *Γραικοί* (Graikoí), originally the name of a small tribe in Boeotia. The Romans likely first encountered the Greeks via the western Greek colonies in southern Italy and generalized this local name to all Greeks. Attested in Latin from Ennius onward (3rd c. BCE) and the source of nearly all modern European exonyms for Greece.
