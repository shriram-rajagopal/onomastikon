The standard Biblical Hebrew name for Egypt, sharing the Semitic *mṣr* root with Akkadian *Miṣru*. The *-ayim* ending is morphologically a dual, traditionally interpreted as referring to Upper and Lower Egypt — though some scholars treat it as a frozen geographic suffix rather than a productive dual. The Tiberian vocalization /misˤˈʁajim/ is the medieval Masoretic tradition; earlier pronunciation cannot be reconstructed with confidence.
