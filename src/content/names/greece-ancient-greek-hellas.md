Originally the name of a small region in Phthia, Thessaly (Homer, *Iliad* 2.683–684), the term gradually extended to all Greek-speakers and became the pan-Hellenic self-designation by the 6th c. BCE. Thucydides offers an explicit ancient discussion of the term's semantic expansion. The ultimate etymology of *Hellas* is uncertain and may be pre-Greek.
