The Parthians' own name for themselves and their land, *Parθaw*, in the Parthian language of the Arsacid empire, written in the Inscriptional Parthian script. It continues the Old Persian *Parθava* directly, the northeastern Iranian form as spoken by the people it named, the Parni and the Arsacid house that ruled from the Parthian homeland for nearly five centuries.

*Parθaw* is the endonym proper, the form on the lips of the Parthians at the height of their power, when their cavalry held the Roman legions on the Euphrates. It is also the pivot of the page's central transmission: it is from this *Parθaw*, not from the Old Persian, that the Middle Persian *Pahlaw* develops by the regular Iranian softening of *rθ* to *hl*, and so the Parthians' name for themselves becomes, a few centuries on, the word *Pahlavi*.
