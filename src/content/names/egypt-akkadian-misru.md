The standard Akkadian name for Egypt, especially frequent in Neo-Assyrian and Neo-Babylonian sources. Variant spellings include *Muṣur* and *Muṣru*. The name derives from a Semitic root *mṣr* meaning "border" or "fortified place," shared with Hebrew *Miṣrayim* and other Northwest Semitic forms. The geographic determinative KUR ("land") regularly precedes the name in cuneiform.
