Same Ionian root as Old Persian *Yauna* and Sanskrit *Yavana*, transmitted via Northwest Semitic. Genesis 10:2 lists Yāwān among the sons of Japheth in the Table of Nations, reflecting Greek presence in the Eastern Mediterranean known to ancient Israel.
