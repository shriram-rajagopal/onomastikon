The earliest attested Greek name for Egypt, appearing in Homer (*Odyssey* 4.83) and continuous through the entire Greek tradition. The prevailing etymology derives *Aígyptos* from Egyptian *Ḥwt-kꜣ-Ptḥ*, "House of the *ka* of Ptah," a cult-name for Memphis — the capital's name was generalized to the whole country. The derivation is plausible and widely accepted but not universally so; alternative etymologies have been proposed.
