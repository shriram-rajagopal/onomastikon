The most prominent Egyptian name for the Nile, though strictly speaking *Ḥꜥpy* names the annual inundation as a deity rather than the river itself as a geographic feature. Egyptians more commonly used *jtrw* ("the river") for the watercourse and reserved *Ḥꜥpy* for the personified flood, depicted in iconography as an androgynous figure with pendulous breasts symbolizing fertility. The Egyptian distinction between river-as-geography and river-as-divine-force has no clean parallel in Greek or Latin.
